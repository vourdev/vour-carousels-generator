For your projects

How to start using
this for yourself

1
Start minimal
Your colors (with descriptions of when to use each), your fonts, and 3 hierarchy rules. Even that beats prompting from scratch.
2
Write what NOT to do
Claude follows constraints better than instructions. "Don't use more than 2 font weights" works better than "use variety thoughtfully."
3
Swap and test
Change the palette, type pairing, or radius scale and regenerate. Fastest way to explore visual directions.
